## Short courses worth doing before July 2027
These came up repeatedly across the batches:
- **ICH-GCP certificate** (free online, e.g. NIDA): needed for clinical research and drug-safety roles.
- **GAMP 5 and data-integrity primer:** for QA and validation roles.
- **Benchling, SQL and Python basics:** for lab informatics.
- **Cochrane systematic-review course:** for health economics and evidence synthesis.
- **GHG Protocol / LCA intro:** for sustainability roles.
- **HACCP Level 3 or an introductory auditing course:** for food-safety and audit roles.
- **A writing portfolio:** a plain-language summary of your phycocyanin thesis, useful for medical writing, publishing and consulting applications.
