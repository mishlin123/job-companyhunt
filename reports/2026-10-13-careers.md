## Things to know about these routes
- **Patent firms:** UK firms run yearly trainee intakes, but only Carpmaels' biotech stream is open now. Most ANZ and Irish firms hire as vacancies come up and welcome speculative CVs. Several firms' biotech teams lean towards PhDs: D Young, FRKelly, Phillips Ormonde Fitzpatrick (which says a PhD is "very helpful") and Withers & Rogers. Mewburn Ellis, Marks & Clerk, HGF, J A Kemp and Dehns say no PhD is needed.
- **Government:** UK Civil Service roles are fine for you. UKHSA and APHA lab posts need a counter-terrorist security check, which normally needs 3 years' UK residency. Australian public-service and security-cleared posts usually need Australian citizenship. NZ has no issue.
- **Equipment makers:** field application roles were all experienced hires this week. The entry routes are graduate programmes: Sartorius has run an ANZ graduate trainee scheme in the past, Thermo's ANZ programme is mostly commercial, and Merck's graduate schemes are coming up. Cytiva's Portsmouth Scientist I roles show there is entry-level lab work inside these companies.
- **AlgoSource**, the spirulina and phycocyanin producer closest to your thesis, has no vacancies and no careers page. It's a 25-person firm in Guérande. A speculative note to its R&D lead is the only route.
