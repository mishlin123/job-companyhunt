# Analyst and project roles that fit — 3 Nov 2026

A one-off look beyond bench and graduate-scientist roles, at analyst and project-coordination jobs. Source: a SEEK API sweep (NZ and AU, last 30 days, 33 analyst/project keywords, about 7,900 listings) filtered to science, agri-food, health research and government employers, plus a check of graduate-intake timing.

## Why these fit you

The evidence for analyst and project work is already in what you've done, not just the MSc:

- **Techno-economic and cost modelling** (profile: "have"), which most science graduates can't show.
- **Data and code**: Python tools you built and shipped (the SEEK and careers scanners in this repo, a stock-call tracker against the S&P 500, a bank-feed budget app, a tender scanner).
- **Running projects**: you plan and coordinate multi-stream builds and keep the docs current. Frame it as project coordination, with numbers.
- **Domain**: precision fermentation at Daisy Lab plus a Galdieria/phycocyanin thesis. In an analyst seat that becomes "the person who understands the science".

## Timing, as for every other route

Every SEEK listing below wants someone now. Until about March 2027 they are signals (who hires, what they ask for, who to contact). From about April 2027 the same kinds of ads become real options. The roles that actually start after 1 July 2027 are the **graduate intakes**, and their windows are the thing to put in the calendar:

| Intake | Start | Applications | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| NZ public-service policy and analyst graduate programmes (MPI, MBIE, MSD, Education, Treasury, Ministry for Regulation) | Feb 2028 | about **June to August 2027** | The 2027 rounds closed in Aug 2026 (MPI closed 9 Aug; MBIE procurement 17 Jul to 17 Aug). MPI is the strongest fit: biosecurity, food safety, primary-sector science |
| Australian Government Graduate Program, Data and STEM streams (incl. DAFF, Industry, Health) | Feb 2028 | opens about **March 2027** | Data stream run by the ABS, 200+ roles. NZ citizens: check each agency's citizenship rule, many APS roles need Australian citizenship (a blocker under your rules) |
| EPA Gene Technology Regulator build-out | rolling | watch careers.epa.govt.nz | Currently senior hires only, but a new regulator for exactly your field. Good speculative target for an advisor or analyst role from mid-2027 |

## Role families, best fit first

### 1. Science and primary-sector policy analyst (strong)
Gene-technology reform and novel foods are live policy in NZ right now, and very few graduate policy analysts can speak precision fermentation.
- MPI graduate programme (Feb 2028 intake), MBIE science-system and gene-tech teams, EPA new organisms / gene tech regulator.
- Live signals: [Policy Analyst, MSD](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95562987) (pitched at emerging analysts), [Advisor, Ministry of Health](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95630556), [Assistant Analyst, Policy, NSW Office of AI](https://www.seek.com.au/job/95624179).

### 2. Research project coordinator / project officer at research organisations (strong)
The cleanest bridge from the lab: you run the project, not the bench.
- [Project Coordinator, viral vector manufacturing project, QIMR Berghofer](https://www.seek.com.au/job/95493004) (Brisbane). Biomanufacturing project, a close match.
- [Research Contracts & Project Officer, a national CRC](https://www.seek.com.au/job/95536358) (Adelaide, via Perks People).
- [Project Delivery Manager, Bioeconomy Science Institute](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95542563) and [Project Delivery Manager, NZ Food Innovation Network](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95335575): senior, so signals. NZFIN's Canterbury hub is FoodSouth at Lincoln, worth a coffee and a possible study-time foothold.
- [Research Operation Coordinator, University of Auckland](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95517891).

### 3. Agri-food and science data analyst (strong)
- [Science Data Analyst, Dairy Trust Taranaki](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95303112): agricultural science plus data plus research support. Close to ideal shape.
- [Graduate Data Analyst, data economics and agriculture](https://www.seek.com.au/job/95574995) (Sydney, via Halcyon Knights): graduate entry.
- [Associate Business Analyst, data science platforms (APS5)](https://www.seek.com.au/job/95601669) (Melbourne): federal, check citizenship.
- [Data Analyst, Hill's Pet Nutrition](https://www.seek.com.au/job/95325905), [Business Analyst, Zespri](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95390713): commercial side of food, possible.

### 4. Agribusiness, biotech and investment analyst (possible, high upside)
Your techno-economic modelling is the edge here: you can tell a fund whether a fermentation process will ever hit its cost target.
- [Agribusiness Analyst, Aspire Agri](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95533571) (Dunedin).
- [Analyst - Investments, Duxton Capital](https://www.seek.com.au/job/95562801) (Adelaide, agri investment).
- Not on SEEK, approach directly: NZ deep-tech and agrifood investors (Pacific Channel, NZ Growth Capital Partners, WNT Ventures, AgriZeroNZ) and AU ones (Main Sequence, Breakthrough Victoria). Treat as speculative "available from July 2027" notes.

### 5. Innovation and commercialisation advisor (possible)
- [Business Growth and Innovation Advisor, Development West Coast](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95562180), [Innovation Navigator, Northland Inc](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95542305): regional business-growth advisors who broker R&D funding. Canterbury equivalent is ChristchurchNZ.

### 6. Technical and capital project coordination in food and dairy manufacturing (signal)
Mostly want project or engineering experience, and sit close to your ruled-out manufacturing-operations line. Useful only as signals of who's building plants.
- [Project Coordinator, Group Automation and Innovation](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95592544) (East Tāmaki, 19 capital projects), [Project Manager, Oceania Dairy](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95223114) (Glenavy), [Project Manager, Olam Food Ingredients](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95406027).

Inside your "clinical research operations" route but next to the CRA rule-out. [Start Up Coordinator, Optimal Clinical Trials](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95474474) (Auckland), [Clinical Research Coordinator, PCRN Trials](https://www.seek.co.nz/job/95638028) (Christchurch).
