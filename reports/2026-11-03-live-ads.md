# Live ads to apply to: 3 Nov 2026

From a live-ads sweep of SEEK (Australia and NZ, last 14 days) and the new UK search (Reed, NHS Jobs, NHS Scotland, HSC Northern Ireland and LinkedIn, last 14 days; England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland). About 1,060 matching listings were screened against `profile.md`; these 62 made the cut and are now in the tracker, so they show in the next report's "Apply this week" list and on the job board after Monday's scan.

Every one is a live ad that wants someone sooner than July 2027. Apply anyway, saying you're available full-time from 1 July 2027 and finishing an MSc in Microbiology. Larger employers, universities, government bodies and utilities are likelier to wait or to come back to you; agency ads rarely do.

Left out: ads saying immediate start, fixed-term or seasonal roles, senior roles, roles needing clinical registration (medical laboratory and biomedical scientists), chemistry and analytical QC, environmental consulting, food NPD and plant-FSQ graduate roles, and Australian government posts needing citizenship.
