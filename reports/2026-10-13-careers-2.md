## What each route looks like for you
| Route | Fit for you | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Patent examiner** | Medium | UK citizens qualify for the EPO (Master's plus C1 English, with French and German to B2 later; €7–10k a month net), but this round has no biotech field. The UK IPO's 2026 intake had no biology stream. IPONZ's science team hires biotech graduates when vacancies arise. **IP Australia: Australian citizens only.** |
| **R&D tax** | High in the UK | Big Four and mid-tier firms run September graduate intakes that take science degrees and pay you to qualify in tax. In Australia, the RSM, Grant Thornton and BDO R&D tax graduate rounds have closed, and some say AU citizens or PR only, so check whether NZ citizens qualify. In NZ, EY's R&D tax team hires science graduates. |
| **Regulatory affairs** | High | The strongest are graduate schemes (Kerry, AbbVie, HPRA, HSE and MHRA's scheme, which opens again in early 2027). Consultancies such as Intertek, Ramboll and SGS had no entry roles. |
| **Medical writing** | High in the UK | Costello, Inizio's allegro.WRITE and Omnicom's Fuel programme take 2:1 science graduates with no experience. Most other agencies prefer PhDs or experience. |
| **Strategy consulting** | High in the UK | L.E.K., ClearView, ZS, Kx and DeciBio all have 2027 analyst intakes open to MSc graduates. Nothing similar is open in Australia or NZ. |
| **Tech transfer / publishing** | Medium | The LifeArc fellowship (opens around January) and KiwiNet / AUT Ventures internships are the routes in. MDPI hires Master's graduates as assistant editors; Nature, Cell Press and PLOS editors need a PhD. |
| **Food safety / audit** | Medium | Graduate schemes (Campden BRI, Kepak) are the way in; certification bodies want auditors with years of experience. Standards Australia and the TGA route need Australian citizenship. |
